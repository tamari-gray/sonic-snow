    Altitude

    Refetched automatically after every point placed, dragged, or deleted. Uses LINZ's surveyed Queenstown LiDAR (1 m grid, ~0.2 m accuracy) where a point falls inside the survey area, and falls back to a coarser global terrain grid (~30 m, quantised to whole metres) outside it — the per‑point source is shown below. On routes with too little relief to trust, altitudes are sent as zero and the game places everything flat at start‑gate height, which is correct for a flat route.
